* Simula: Speeding Up Simulation with AI
  Total time: ~4 min. The demo is about half of it.

** Slide 1: Hook + Title                                      (0:00–0:20)
   - Visual: 5-second side-by-side clip
     - Left: solver, still computing (timer running)
     - Right: Simula, tsunami already hitting the island
   - Title: *Simula: Speeding up simulation with AI*
   - Say: "Same physics. One of these took a second."

** Slide 2: The Problem                                       (0:20–0:50)
   - Two stakes, one picture each
     - *Tsunami*: an earthquake hits, and forecasters have minutes to decide who evacuates
     - *Heart*: a surgeon has dozens of possible ablation sites, and each one needs a simulation
   - Punchline: *Simulation is too slow for decisions that can't wait*

** Slide 3: Why AI Hasn't Fixed It                           (0:50–1:10)
   - AI shortcuts are fast...
   - ...but they need *thousands* of simulations to learn each new problem
   - Visual: a big pile of "simulations needed" for every new physics problem
   - Punchline: *The training cost just moves the bottleneck*

** Slide 4: Simula                                            (1:10–1:40)
   - Learned from *5,000+ kinds of physics* before seeing your problem
   - Reads your equation and starts with an educated guess
   - Runs only the *few* simulations that teach it the most
   - Visual: 3 icons → "Learned physics" → "Reads your equation" → "Picks smart experiments"
   - Punchline: *A handful of simulations instead of thousands*

** Slide 5: Live Demo                                         (1:40–3:10)
   - Tsunami
     - Drag the epicenter → instant prediction vs. solver still running
     - 1,000-earthquake flood-risk map in ~1 second
   - Heart
     - Place a scar → watch a spiral (arrhythmia) form instantly
     - Try ablation sites → see which one stops it
   - Backup: pre-recorded video if anything fails

** Slide 6: Results                                           (3:10–3:35)
